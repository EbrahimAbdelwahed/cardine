# Heart valves

This sanitized fixture contains general anatomy facts for the offline demo.

- The tricuspid valve sits between the right atrium and the right ventricle.
- The pulmonary valve sits between the right ventricle and the pulmonary trunk.
- The mitral valve sits between the left atrium and the left ventricle.
- The aortic valve sits between the left ventricle and the aorta.

Pressure differences across each valve determine when it opens and closes. The
valves support forward blood flow; they do not actively pump blood.
